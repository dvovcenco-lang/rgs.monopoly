✦FAQ

Questions people ask us

Everything about paying, collecting and the game itself.

Rather order in person?

Come to our stall at the Christmas fair and pay cash, while sets last. Or email us at rgs.monopoly@gmail.com.

How much is it?

£20 a set.

How do I pay?

Online by card when you order. Your order is confirmed once we've received your payment. Or skip ordering and pay cash at our stall at the fair, while sets last.

When and where do I collect?

From the Tycoon Enterprise stall at the Christmas fair on Sun 6 Dec. Give us the name on your order or your reference number.

When do orders close?

Fri 4 Dec, 6pm. After that, any spare sets are sold at the stall on the day.

Does it play like the normal game?

Yes. Same rules, same money, same board layout. The squares and cards are all about RGS.

Can you tell me the squares?

Nope. That's half the fun. You'll find out when you open the box.

How many are there?

Not many. Once they're gone, they're gone, so reserve early.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes. Open Order from the menu and you'll see your reservation with buttons to change or cancel it.

Do I need to give my email?

No, it's optional. If you add an email or phone number, we'll only use it to tell you when your set is ready.
